## The problem

Nursing students and staff needed a manageable way to enter complex clinical training data. Dense forms and ad-hoc spreadsheets made patient profiles hard to build and easy to get wrong. The product had to feel clear to non-technical users without watering down the clinical structure.

## Role

I was frontend lead on a student team building the CARE training SPA. I owned the component architecture for patient profile builders and multi-step forms, kept API contracts aligned with the backend sub-team, and co-delivered the MVP pitch to healthcare administration.

## Approach

The frontend is a React SPA organized around reusable profile sections and typed multi-step flows. The goal was to keep clinical data entry guided and scannable: one job per step, clear progress, and components that nursing staff could actually complete under time pressure.

Additional stills from the profile builder and form flows are coming once screens are scrubbed of any sensitive training data.

## Hard parts

- **Domain UX:** Translating clinical structure into a UI that non-technical staff can finish without getting lost.
- **Team liaison:** Keeping frontend and backend hand-offs documented so API contracts matched real user flows.
- **Stakeholder pitch:** Helping present an MVP that administrators could fund and put on a curriculum roadmap.

## Results

Awarded 2024-2025 Student of the Year for technical contribution on CARE. Co-delivered the MVP pitch that helped secure continued funding and a Fall 2026 production pilot in the nursing curriculum. Placed 2nd in the college's first annual hackathon the same year.

## Reflection

CARE taught me that frontend leadership is as much communication as components: clear contracts, usable flows, and a story stakeholders can say yes to. More product stills will land here as scrubbed screenshots are ready.
